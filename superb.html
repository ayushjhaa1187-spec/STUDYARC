SkillBridge Pro

An AI career execution platform — full concept, workflows, revenue model, build systems, and launch offer, in one place for the team.

01

Platform concept

Build this as an AI Career Execution Platform — not another course marketplace. It should take a student from "I want a job / freelance opportunity / skill" to a verified outcome: a learning plan, projects, portfolio, expert feedback, and relevant paid opportunities. The essential startup rule: start with one narrow user segment and validate an MVP before building a wide platform.

Initial niche — do not start broad

Indian college students and freshers who want to become job-ready for data, AI, full-stack, or product-development roles.

Why this is the right wedge

  • You understand the user personally as an IIT Madras data-science student.
  • You already have a live SkillBridge foundation using Next.js, Supabase, TypeScript, and Gemini.
  • Your hackathon / IIT network gives you a low-cost first distribution channel.
  • The target user has a clear measurable outcome: portfolio, internship, freelance gig, interview readiness, or first income.

Do not begin with: school education, all career fields, all mentors, or all professional services. Win one urgent use case first — "help technical students build a credible AI/full-stack portfolio in 30 days."

How the three models combine

Astrotalk's model centers on paid human-expert interactions, Coursera combines consumer credentials with enterprise access, and Udemy combines an instructor marketplace with a large recurring enterprise business. Borrow the mechanisms — not the exact products.

flowchart TB A[Astrotalk principle] --> A1[Match a user with a trusted\nhuman expert at a high-intent moment] A1 --> A2[Charge per interaction\nand pay expert share] C[Coursera principle] --> C1[Structured pathways\nand outcome proof] C1 --> C2[Subscription / certificate /\ninstitutional revenue] Ud[Udemy principle] --> U1[Fast practical content\nfrom domain practitioners] U1 --> U2[Marketplace supply and\nlearner discovery] A2 --> S[SkillBridge Pro] C2 --> S U2 --> S S --> X[AI-led execution pathway\n+ human escalation\n+ verified portfolio outcome]

Company insight → your adaptation

Company insightYour adaptation
Astrotalk: users pay when they urgently need personalized helpTrigger expert support only when the learner is blocked, preparing for an interview, reviewing a resume, or shipping a project
Coursera: users value structured progression and credible proofGive every sprint a clear outcome, rubric, completion proof, and portfolio artifact
Udemy: practical content supply can scale quicklyLet strong builders create reusable "project blueprints," but curate heavily in the beginning
All three: trust, discovery, payment, quality, and retention are core systemsBuild these systems before trying to scale content or mentor supply

User journey

From landing page to a verified portfolio card and a paid upgrade — the path a real learner walks through the product.

flowchart TD A[Landing page:\nChoose a career goal] --> B[5-minute AI diagnostic] B --> C[Free result:\nSkill-gap map + 7-day starter plan] C --> D[Start free challenge:\nBuild one portfolio artifact] D --> E{Progressing?} E -- Yes --> F[AI coach gives feedback,\ntasks and next project] E -- Stuck / high stakes --> G[Book verified expert\n15–30 min focused review] F --> H[Project submitted] G --> H H --> I[Automated + human rubric review] I --> J[Verified portfolio card,\nGitHub proof, shareable profile] J --> K[Upgrade to Pro /\nnext career sprint] K --> L[Referral, peer group,\nnew user acquisition]
Example — first paying user

Aman, second-year BTech student, wants an AI internship but has no projects.

  1. He completes the free "AI Internship Readiness Scan."
  2. SkillBridge identifies weak GitHub evidence and missing end-to-end projects.
  3. He gets a free 7-day sprint: build and deploy a document-Q&A app.
  4. He upgrades for ₹299–₹499/month to receive daily AI guidance, code reviews, and a portfolio pathway.
  5. When preparing to apply, he books a ₹299–₹599 expert resume/GitHub review.
  6. He earns a verified project card and applies through curated opportunity challenges.

The platform makes money without needing to promise employment. You sell job-readiness execution and evidence, not a guaranteed job.

07

MVP scope

Version 1 is one straight line from goal onboarding to a paid expert review — nothing wider.

flowchart LR A[Goal onboarding] --> B[AI diagnostic] B --> C[30-day sprint generator] C --> D[Daily task dashboard] D --> E[AI feedback and help] E --> F[Project submission] F --> G[Public portfolio card] G --> H[Expert review booking] H --> I[Payment and rating]

Must-have features

  • Goal selection: "AI internship," "full-stack portfolio," "data analyst readiness," or "freelance web developer"
  • AI diagnostic based on skills, time availability, experience, resume/GitHub links, and intended outcome
  • One focused 30-day execution sprint per goal
  • Daily tasks, streaks, milestone deadlines, and AI feedback
  • Project templates with a clear rubric and deployment/GitHub submission
  • Public portfolio profile with verified project cards
  • Expert profile, availability, booking, payment, feedback, and payout tracking
  • Admin dashboard for mentor approval, disputes, project reviews, revenue, and user metrics

Explicitly postpone

  • Native Android / iOS apps
  • Live group classes
  • Full creator course-upload marketplace
  • Large community forum
  • Job guarantee
  • AI-generated certificates without rigorous evidence
  • Hundreds of mentors or many categories
  • Complex recommender models

A broad e-learning product typically requires planning, MVP validation, testing, and iterative improvement — keep the first version narrowly outcome-focused rather than attempting the full feature set of a mature marketplace.

Growth loop

Every finished project is a public artifact. Every public artifact is a distribution channel.

flowchart LR A[Free diagnostic and\ncareer-gap report] --> B[Student starts sprint] B --> C[Builds visible portfolio artifact] C --> D[Shares result on LinkedIn,\nGitHub, campus group] D --> E[New student sign-ups] E --> F[More learner data] F --> G[Better AI plans,\nmatching and projects] G --> B B --> H[Paid Pro / expert review] H --> I[Revenue] I --> J[Recruit better experts,\nimprove verification] J --> G

Low-cost acquisition channels

  • IIT, NPTEL, hackathon, campus-ambassador, and developer-community networks
  • LinkedIn posts showing real project transformations, not generic "learn AI" content
  • Open-source project templates with an embedded SkillBridge portfolio-validation CTA
  • Student referral rewards: free expert-review credit, not cash initially
  • College clubs and coding societies: run a "Build and Deploy in 7 Days" event
  • Free career diagnostic as the lead magnet

Trust, quality, and safety

Expert quality is enforced as a loop, not a one-time approval.

flowchart LR A[Expert application] --> B[Identity + LinkedIn /\nGitHub verification] B --> C[Interview and trial review] C --> D[Approved expert profile] D --> E[Session logs, ratings,\ncomplaints and quality score] E --> F{Quality threshold met?} F -- Yes --> D F -- No --> G[Coaching, pause or removal]

Strict boundaries

  • Do not claim the service guarantees employment, admission, income, or freelance work
  • Show what is AI-generated, what is mentor-reviewed, and what is verified from external evidence
  • Obtain consent before displaying GitHub, portfolio, resume, or session data publicly
  • Do not let mentors give legal, medical, financial, or mental-health advice unless properly qualified and the category is designed for it
  • Start with well-defined professional support: project reviews, technical mentoring, resume feedback, interview practice, portfolio evaluation

Your first launch offer

"AI Portfolio Sprint: Build, Deploy, and Get Reviewed in 30 Days"

Target: Indian technical students/freshers who have learned the basics but lack a strong proof-of-work portfolio.

Includes

  • AI career diagnostic
  • Personalized 30-day roadmap
  • One end-to-end deployed AI/full-stack project
  • Daily AI guidance and debugging support
  • GitHub / readme / deployment checklist
  • One expert project or resume review
  • Verified public portfolio card
  • Demo-day / community showcase

Founding price: ₹399–₹799 for the first cohort, depending on how much human review is included. Narrow enough to sell, build, and measure — after 20–30 successful completions, expand into data analytics, UI/UX, product management, freelancing, and other professional-service pathways.

Your real moat will not be Gemini prompts or course videos. It will be the outcome graph — data showing which learner profile, daily actions, expert interventions, and project types reliably lead to a finished portfolio and career progress.
